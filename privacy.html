Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07

Lesson Transcription is a private tool used only by its owner. It has no other users.

What data it accesses

  • Google Drive: reads audio and video recordings available to the owner, and creates transcript and summary files in a folder in the owner's Drive.
  • Gmail: sends notification emails to the owner only. It does not read, modify or delete any email.

How data is used

Recordings are sent to OpenAI's API for transcription and summarization. The results are stored only in the owner's Google Drive. No data is sold, shared with or shown to any other person or service.

Retention

Temporary audio files are deleted at the end of each run. Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account settings.
